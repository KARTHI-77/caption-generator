Privacy Policy - Caption Generator

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Caption Generator is an AI-powered application that generates captions for photos. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

2. Information We Handle

  • Account information, such as your name and email when you sign in with Google.
  • Photos submitted for caption generation.
  • Generated captions, selected styles, languages, and caption history.
  • Technical information processed by service providers to operate and secure the app.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate users, generate captions, save caption history, and operate and maintain the app.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Sign-In, Firebase, backend hosting, and an AI service for caption generation. Their applicable privacy policies apply to information they process.

5. Photos and AI Processing

Photos submitted for caption generation may be transmitted to the app's backend and AI provider for processing. Avoid uploading sensitive photos unless you are comfortable with this processing.

6. Data Storage and Security

Account information and caption history may be stored using Firebase services. Reasonable measures are taken to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Service providers may process information as necessary to provide app functionality, generate captions, and maintain security.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as necessary to provide the service. Users can delete saved captions using the available app features. For other privacy or data deletion requests, contact us below. Deleting a caption may not delete copies retained by service providers.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

10. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change.
