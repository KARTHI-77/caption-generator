# Privacy Policy — Caption Generator

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Caption Generator is an AI-powered application that helps users generate captions for their photos. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

## 2. Information We Handle
Depending on how you use the app, we may process:
- **Account information:** Your name, email address, and authentication information when you sign in using Google.
- **Photos:** Images you select and submit for caption generation.
- **Generated content:** Captions, selected styles, languages, and caption history saved through the app.
- **Technical information:** Information processed by the app's hosting, authentication, and AI service providers to operate and secure the service.

## 3. How We Use Information
Information is used to authenticate users, generate captions, save and display caption history, provide app functionality, and maintain service security and reliability.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Caption Generator uses third-party services to provide its functionality, which may include Google Sign-In, Firebase, backend hosting, and an AI caption-generation provider. Information sent to these services is subject to their applicable privacy policies and practices.

## 5. Photos and AI Processing
Photos submitted for caption generation may be transmitted to our backend and AI service provider for processing. Users should avoid uploading photos containing sensitive or confidential information unless they are comfortable with the applicable processing.

## 6. Data Storage and Security
Account information and caption history may be stored using Firebase services. Reasonable measures are taken to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 7. Data Sharing
We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the application, provide caption generation, or maintain security.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion
Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the service and meet applicable requirements. Users can remove individual saved captions using available in-app deletion features. For requests concerning account information or other stored data, contact us using the email address below.

Deleting a caption from the app may not automatically delete information retained by third-party service providers.

## 9. Children's Privacy
Caption Generator is not specifically directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated when the app's features, services, or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
